01 / About

Bio

A performer who likes to work in more than one language of theatre.

MarcAntonio Murillo, also known as Magic Marc, is an actor, writer, and theatre artist studying Psychology with a minor in Theatre at Northern Arizona University. He has performed onstage since age twelve, building a foundation in comedy, character work, improvisation, and musical theatre.

His recent NAU credits include The Imaginary Invalid, Lysistrata, and Oedipus El Rey. His creative practice also includes stage magic, mascot performance, public speaking, technical theatre, and digital content creation.

✦
A trick up his sleeve

Magic Marc brings sleight of hand, quick comic timing, and a love of surprise to every room. He turns ordinary moments into something audiences remember.
